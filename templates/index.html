The Algorithmic Shift: An Existential Threat to Scholarly Integrity

CPE
Department of Computer Engineering
Official Project Deployment

The landscape of global higher education has crossed a critical technological threshold. The era of simple information retrieval is over; we have abruptly entered the age of information generation. While sophisticated Large Language Models offer unprecedented speed for synthesizing data, debugging complex code arrays, and articulating research, they carry a hidden cost.

They are fundamentally disrupting the traditional academic workflow and shifting the cognitive burden away from the human intellect.

Systemic Vulnerabilities

The unregulated deployment of generative AI has outpaced institutional safeguards. Traditional plagiarism detection systems are functionally obsolete because they rely almost entirely on exact string-matching algorithms, rendering them blind to generative AI. Furthermore, academic supervisors are currently burdened with the impossible, manual task of reading through voluminous submissions to identify AI-induced hallucinations, creating a severe bottleneck in departmental workflows.

The Technological Countermeasure

To bridge the widening gap between algorithmic advancement and academic regulation, manual verification is no longer sufficient. This platform serves as the dedicated technological intervention for the Department of Computer Engineering. It is designed to automatically filter project submissions, streamline supervisor workflows, and rigorously enforce the academic standards of final-year projects in the era of generative AI.
